# RND seeds (bs2_rnd.c): the RND Synth's seeds captured, kept in their own table through power-off, sent back; the

# tools/build.py bs2 --host, then from the repository's root
#   bs2/build/host/bass-something-2_host bs2/tests/rnd.txt bs2/build/host
