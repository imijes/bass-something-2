# Bass Something 2 v0.9.4

Restoring and saving with the organ's ROTOR NOISE on. The full guide is `docs/MANUAL.md`.

- **The organ's rotor wind no longer counts as playing:** with LESLIE NOISES' ROTOR NOISE above 0, the Leslie's wind
  sounds for ever, and the FM-1 took it for playing: a restore said STOP FIRST, a backup was marked "taken while
  playing", and settings kept in the background waited for a silence that never came. The organ now counts as playing
  only while its keys are held, and as ringing only while a note, a click or a clunk rings through its Leslie.
- **STOP FIRST says why:** when a restore is refused, the FM-1's screen names what plays (THE DRUMS RUN, THE SEQ OR ARP
  RUNS, A DRUM RINGS, THE PIANO PLAYS, A BASS NOTE PLAYS, THE BASS RINGS, THE ORGAN PLAYS).

Everything else is as v0.9.3 (`docs/RELEASE_v0.9.3.md`).
