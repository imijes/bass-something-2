# Bass Something 2 v0.9

A fourth instrument, Organ Something B3: a tonewheel organ inspired by the Hammond B-3 with its Leslie 122, made the way the
organ makes it and tuned against a reference B-3 note by note. The full guide is `docs/MANUAL.md` (Organ Something B3).

## Organ Something B3

- **The organ:** 91 tonewheels, nine drawbars, key click, the B-3's single-trigger percussion, the scanner vibrato and
  chorus (V1 to C3, laid out round the knob as on the organ), the preamp's drive and the swell pedal (CC 11, or the
  keys' pressure with KEY SWELL).
- **Two manuals:** upper and lower, each with A# and B drawbar sets as the preset keys pick them; SEL held switches
  manual, EDIT held the preset key, EDIT + OCT+ both keys, EDIT + OCT− held CANCEL. The lower plays on its own MIDI
  channel too.
- **The Leslie:** horn and drum rotors in stereo, chorale, tremolo and the brake (ARP), each rotor's speeds and its
  rise and fall on the 122's trimmers.
- **Noises and mojo:** key thuds, hum, rotor noise and clunks; MOJO and its CHARACTER, from a loose wheel to a barn find.
- **Its pages:** ORGAN HOME, the console from the player's seat; DRAWBARS; the VIBRATO and PERCUSSION cheeks; the LESLIE's
  views (CABINET, ROTORS, HORN ROTOR, LOW ROTOR, AMP, NOISES, MOJO); ORGAN NAME. 32 presets, twelve classic
  registrations.
- **Its part of each drum pattern:** recorded and played as the piano's. PLAY, PLAY held (undo) and SEQ held (clear) act
  on the manual you play alone; the drums' PLAY starts both.
- **Its own mix:** its volume, its echo send, its USB audio channels, its MIDI and CC channels (CC 12 to 31, 9 its
  CHARACTER).

## Everywhere

- **Five HOMEs:** HOME goes round bass, drums, piano, organ and echo. Held a second: the KEYBOARD LOCK keeps the keys on
  one instrument whatever page you're on.
- **Knobs alike:** SELECT is each instrument's own volume on its HOME; every mod page laid out the same (label, knob,
  value, the value popping up as it turns, the hint over the label).
- **GLOBAL CC CHANNELS** (after MIDI CHANNELS) and **GLOBAL MOTION** (after DISPLAY), the motion recorder's mods.
- **AFTERTOUCH:** its knobs on K1 to K4, K4 KEYS FROM (BASS, BASS+PIANO, BASS+ORGAN, ALL).
- **SAVE** as a Bass Station II's: the slot blinking, PRESET picks, SAVE stores.
- **Lower latency:** audio blocks of 2.9 ms, a MIDI note to its sound in about 5.6 ms (was 10.2).
- **Overload guard:** when the FM-1 runs short of time it thins the quietest voices rather than crackle.
- **PIANO HOME:** the voice at the top right, a line each kind.
- **Random sounds** kept musical.

The firmware is 531,252 B, with about 50 KB of flash and 17 KB of RAM free.
