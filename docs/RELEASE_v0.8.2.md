# Bass Something 2 v0.8.2

The drums and the piano part start and stop on their own, and the Space Echo's presets on its panel. The full guide is
`docs/MANUAL.md`.

## Transport

- **With TRANSPORT off (DRUM MODS, K4), each instrument stops on its own:** the drums' PLAY stops only the drums while
  the piano part plays on (DRUMS STOPPED); pressed again, the drums come back in on the beat (DRUMS PLAYING).
- **The piano's PLAY starts and stops its part alone:** with the drums stopped it starts the piano part by itself
  (PIANO PART PLAYING) and stops it (PIANO PART STOPPED). With the drums playing it turns the part off and on, as before.
- With TRANSPORT on, the drums' PLAY still starts and stops everything.

## Space Echo

- **ECHO PANEL:** PRESET picks the Space Echo's presets (MODE), as on HOME; BASS moved to SELECT.

The firmware is 466,928 B, with about 115 KB of flash and 25 KB of RAM free.
