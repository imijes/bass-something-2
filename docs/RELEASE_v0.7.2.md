# Bass Something 2 v0.7.2

The first-boot demo is now the author's own groove, "i've used up 94% of my usage tokens", saved on their FM-1: the
bass in P01 with its line in sequence 1, and the drums' pattern 00 (four bars at 120 BPM) with their volumes,
tunings and master level. Hold HOME, press PLAY, and the bass follows the drums (DRUM MODS' TRANSPORT).

The demo only loads on a new FM-1, or on one whose saves are cleared: an update keeps your own patches, sequences
and patterns. The code that built v0.7.1's demo is gone; `presets/make_demo.py` makes the new one from a backup.

Size: 516,496 B (-16), about 65 KB of flash and 19.9 KB of RAM free.
